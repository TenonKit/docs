## The window flickers or stays blank

Turn off **Settings → Performance → Graphics acceleration**, then restart Tenonkit.

## A component shows an error

The message on the canvas and under the code says what broke. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> undoes your last change.

## Reporting a problem

**Settings → About → Logs** opens the log file. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it.

## Coming from Redesign

Tenonkit used to be called Redesign. The first time it starts, it copies your old projects, themes and settings across and leaves the originals untouched.
