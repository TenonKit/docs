## Adding components

Drag a component from the left onto the page, or click it to add it below the one you have selected.

## Arranging

To move a block, drag its handle in the toolbar that appears over it, use the arrows beside it, or reorder it in the **Layers** tab.

## Screen sizes

The buttons at the top switch the canvas width, so you can check every layout in the editor:

- **Desktop**, 1280 px
- **Tablet**, 768 px
- **Phone**, 390 px
