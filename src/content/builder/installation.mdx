## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- About 375 MB of disk space
- No internet connection needed to build

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} title="Download the installer">
    Get **Tenonkit-1.0.0-setup.exe** from the [download page](https://tenonkit.com/download).
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} title="Run it">
    Keep the suggested folder or pick your own. Shortcuts go on the desktop and in the Start menu.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} title="Open Tenonkit">
    It opens when setup finishes. Continue with the [quickstart](/builder/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Windows SmartScreen may say it doesn’t recognise the app, because new installers start without a reputation. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You can check the file’s checksum on the [download page](https://tenonkit.com/download#verify) first.
</Callout>

## Uninstalling

Use **Apps** in Windows Settings. Your projects and themes stay in `%APPDATA%\Tenonkit` in case you come back; delete that folder to remove everything.
