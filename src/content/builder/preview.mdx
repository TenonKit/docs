**Preview** opens your page in your browser from a small server on your own computer. Open preview tabs reload whenever you edit.

## Two modes

- **HTML page** shows exactly what the HTML export will contain.
- **React app** runs your components for real, with state and events.

<Callout>
  The preview server listens on `127.0.0.1` only, so other devices on your network can’t reach it.
</Callout>
